GB Clip

A private tool that posts my own approved gaming clips to my own TikTok account.

🎮 Gaming clips

Short vertical clips of GTA 6 gameplay highlights, with captions.

✅ Approved by hand

Nothing is posted unless I have reviewed and approved the clip first.

🔒 Private & personal

Used only by its owner, with one TikTok account. No public sign-ups.

How it works

  1. Review. I watch each clip and approve the ones I want to post.
  2. Connect. I sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) to give GB Clip permission to upload to my account.
  3. Upload. GB Clip sends approved clips and captions to my TikTok account through the official Content Posting API, spaced out over the day.

Your data

GB Clip only uses basic account info and upload permission from TikTok. Tokens stay on my own computer and are never sold or shared. Details in the Privacy Policy.
