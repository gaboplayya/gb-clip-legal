GB Clip – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

1. Summary

GB Clip is a private tool that uploads the owner's own approved clips to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect information about other people, does not sell or share data, and has no ads or tracking.

2. What GB Clip receives from TikTok

  • Basic account info (the user.info.basic permission): the account's open ID and display name, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok when the owner signs in, used only to upload videos (the video.upload and video.publish permissions).

GB Clip does not read followers, messages, likes, viewing history, contacts or any other TikTok data.

3. How it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private configuration file. They are not stored on any server run by GB Clip, and they are sent only to TikTok's official API.

4. What is uploaded

Only video clips and captions that the owner has reviewed and approved are sent to TikTok, to the owner's account.

5. Sharing

GB Clip does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data goes only to TikTok, as needed to upload the owner's videos.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. To delete them, revoke GB Clip's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services / Manage app permissions). The owner also deletes the local tokens from their computer.

7. Children

GB Clip is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date.
