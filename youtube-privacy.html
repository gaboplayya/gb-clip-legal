GB Clip – Privacy Policy (YouTube)

Effective date: November 19, 2026

GB Clip uses YouTube API Services. By using GB Clip with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

1. What GB Clip is

A private, single-user desktop tool that uploads its owner's own approved short gameplay clips to the owner's own YouTube channel, and reads the view counts of those uploads. It has no other users.

2. What data GB Clip accesses

  • Your channel's basic info (channels.list, mine=true): to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Uploads (videos.insert): only clips the owner approved, with title, description and tags.
  • Statistics of your own uploads (videos.list): views and likes, to see which clips perform best.

GB Clip does not access other users' data, comments, subscribers or watch history.

3. How it is used, stored and shared

Data is used only to upload the owner's clips and show the owner how they perform, on a local page on the owner's own computer. Access tokens and statistics are stored only on that computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and no cookies or tracking are used.

4. Deleting data and revoking access

Revoke GB Clip's access at any time at Google security settings (third-party access). Stored statistics are deleted from the local computer on request or when access is revoked. Data obtained through the YouTube API is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.
